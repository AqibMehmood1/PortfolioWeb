LEGAL COMPLIANCE & DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · NEXVOYS Ltd. Architecture Studio

1. Introduction

NEXVOYS Ltd. ("NEXVOYS", "we", "our", or "us") is an architecture and engineering consultancy serving startups, SMBs, and enterprise clients worldwide. We take data privacy, confidential trade secrets, and compliance with applicable data protection laws (including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and international privacy standards) seriously.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, handle, protect, and process information when you visit our website (nexvoys.com), submit an architecture consultation inquiry, or engage our solutions architecture services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you submit a project inquiry or book a call:

  • Contact Information: Full name, professional work email address, phone number (optional), and company name.
  • Project & Architectural Context: Project requirements, tech stack details, target timeline, budget range, and any high-level architecture documents you choose to provide.
  • Technical Information: Standard, privacy-preserving server logs including IP address, browser type, and referring URL used solely for diagnostic security and load performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data exclusively for legitimate business purposes:

  • To review and respond to your architectural inquiry within one business day.
  • To scope technical feasibility, estimate delivery roadmaps, and schedule architecture strategy sessions.
  • To execute Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to in-depth technical discovery.
  • To provide agreed consulting, engineering, and fractional architecture services.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or project data to third-party data brokers or marketing advertisers under any circumstance.

4. Non-Disclosure & Confidentiality

We treat all client architectural blueprints, database schemas, API specs, business logic, and codebases as strictly confidential. We execute Mutual NDAs prior to accessing private client repositories, staging environments, or production infrastructure.

5. Data Subject Rights (GDPR & International)

Under GDPR and applicable international regulations, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or rectification of incomplete or inaccurate data.
  • Request the deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing was based on consent.

6. Security & Least-Privilege Access

When collaborating on client projects, NEXVOYS operates on zero-trust and least-privilege principles. We mandate hardware-backed multi-factor authentication (MFA), encrypted communications, and ephemeral access credentials that are revoked immediately upon engagement completion.
